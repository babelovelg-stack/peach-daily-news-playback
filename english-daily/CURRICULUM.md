# 每日课程进阶 / Daily progression

## 中文

本课程是 Power Up 3 等级的家庭补充学习，按第二版官方目录的主题推进；不是整套教材或正式课堂的替代。已经从 Unit 2 饮食主题开始，不声称是教材第一课。每天5个新的重点词、新的原书选段和1–2题；旧词回忆放在3分钟回顾内。原书阅读难于孩子当前 RAZ C–D，因此保留看图、中文提示、词汇和语法支持，不据此自动升级孩子等级。

课号1属于历史旧版，不计入新版词库。3–4课是 Unit 2 主题内补充选词；5–7课对应 Unit 3 官方样章的身体与健康词汇。上述两本书只使用已核对的开头公开样章，未取得完整后文；到下一本样章时明确显示新书名，不冒充上一故事的续页。

后续备课沿官方目录继续：先补足 Unit 2 的 who / which / where 与过去式、Unit 3 比较级及 as … as、want/need to；再进入 Unit 4 自然景物、动作与副词，Unit 5 服饰材料与建议，Unit 6 学校与 should / be good at，Unit 7 职业，Unit 8 城市方位，Unit 9 旅行。每个主题按掌握情况展开，不以一天五词就宣称学完一个单元。新课需有新重点词，语法可以在新情景中螺旋复现。

续编任务每天16:00检查库存，目标为未来7课；在 Codex 本地调度可运行时执行。18:00云端邮件仍走原流程。原书来源失效或库存不足时明确报告，不重复旧课；没有可核对原书时停止发送并保留进度。完整原书后文可能需要官方登录、借阅或购买，不能承诺一直免登录获得全文。

官方依据：[第二版目录](https://cambridgelatamcatalogue.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/Power-Up-Level-3-Map-of-the-book.pdf)、[Unit 3样章](https://cambridgelatamcatalogue.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/Level3Unit3.pdf)、[OUP Level 7及样章](https://shop.oupchina.com.hk/en/products/oxford-reading-tree-biff-chip-and-kipper-stories-level-7-mixed-pack-of-6)。

## English

This is supplemental home study aligned with Power Up 3 second-edition themes, beginning at Unit 2, not a replacement for the complete textbook or classes. The table lists the six reviewed lessons, each with five distinct target words and a new two-page official reading selection. Lessons 3–4 extend the food theme; 5–7 use verified Unit 3 body/health vocabulary. Reading support reflects the child's current RAZ C–D baseline rather than assuming equivalence with ORT Level 7.

Both books are opening publisher samples, not complete stories. A different book is labelled explicitly, never presented as missing continuation pages. Later preparation first fills Unit 2 relative-clause/past-tense and Unit 3 comparison/want/need practice, then progresses through nature, clothes/materials, school, jobs, city and travel themes from the official map. Grammar may recur in new contexts; the five target words and reading selection must be new.

A daily 16:00 local Codex task targets a seven-lesson reviewed buffer. Existing 18:00 cloud email delivery remains unchanged. Missing sources are reported; unavailable originals stop delivery without repeating content or advancing progress. Full-book continuation may require official login, borrowing or purchase. No unlimited free full-text access is promised.
